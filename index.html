Boston 2024 Trash Tonnage

In 2024, Bostonians disposed of 186,305 tons of trash (that's about 372,610,000 pounds). On average, each household that participated in Boston's municipal trash program disposed of over 1300 pounds of trash.

METHODOLOGY: According to data from the Massachusetts Department of Environmental Protection's 2024 Municipal Solid Waste and Recycling Survey, 322,000 households participated in Boston's municipal trash program. Collectively, these households disposed of 186,305 tons of trash, including bulky waste. We divided the total tonnage by the number of participating households to calculate the average weight of trash disposed per household.

Boston 2024 Tipping Fee

In 2024, Boston spent $19,260,210.90 on incinerating trash.
