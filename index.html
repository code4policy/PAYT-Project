Spending Methodology: According to data from the Massachusetts Department of Environmental Protection's 2024 Municipal Solid Waste and Recycling Survey, 322,000 households participated in Boston's municipal trash program. We divided the total tonnage by the number of participating households to calculate the average weight of trash disposed per household.
