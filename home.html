Pay as You Throw Fact Sheet

Pay-As-You-Throw (PAYT) is a solid waste program where residents pay a per-unit fee to dispose of the solid waste they generate.

Why Implement a PAYT Program?

MassDEP has found that PAYT is the most effective mechanism for encouraging waste reduction at the municipal level.
